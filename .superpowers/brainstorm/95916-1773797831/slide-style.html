What visual quality level are you targeting?

Think about the best presentations you've seen at work — where on this spectrum should your weekly deck land?

A

Clean Corporate

Think well-structured consulting slides — clear hierarchy, consistent colors, good typography. No flashiness, just professional and scannable. Like a polished internal report.

B

Modern Tech Company

Think Stripe or Linear's blog posts turned into slides — bold KPI numbers, subtle gradients, icon accents, generous whitespace. Feels like a product you'd want to use.

C

Data Dashboard

Think Datadog or Grafana — dense with information, multiple small charts, status indicators, sparklines. Maximizes data per square inch. Audience reads it more than listens to it.

D

Keynote Quality

Think Apple keynote — one big idea per slide, dramatic visuals, minimal text. Each slide is a statement. Needs a presenter to carry the narrative.
