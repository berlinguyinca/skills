Key visual principles:

  • Headline slide — dark background for impact, KPIs as visual anchors
  • Theme slides — white background, left evidence / right visual anchor (before/after, metric, or timeline)
  • What's Next — status pills + one-liners, no paragraphs
  • Consistent accent bar on every slide for brand continuity
  • No charts for the sake of charts — only visuals that add insight
